Common questions

How do I reserve a place at an event?
Open the event and tap Reserve. Your request starts as pending — the organiser approves or declines it, and you get a notification either way. Some events do not take reservations and simply show the details.
My reservation is still pending.
Reservations are confirmed by the organiser, not automatically. If an event is close and you have not heard back, contact the organiser from the event page, or write to us and we will follow it up.
How do I publish my own events?
Open the Profile tab and apply to become an organiser. Complete your profile first — the application needs a short bio. Applications are reviewed manually, so please allow a little time. Once approved, you publish and manage events from the Manage tab.
I published an event but I cannot find it in the Events tab.
Every event is reviewed before it goes public. Until it is approved it stays visible only to you, under Manage → My events, marked as pending.
What does Dance Addict Pro include?
A free organiser can have one upcoming event at a time. Pro removes that limit and unlocks recurring events, so a weekly social is published once.
How do I cancel Pro?
Pro is an auto-renewable subscription billed through your Apple ID. Manage or cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal. We cannot cancel or refund Apple subscriptions on your behalf — refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, Pro is billed through Google Play: open the Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions to manage or cancel it. Refunds are requested from Google Play.
Something in the app is offensive or misleading.
Use Report on the event or profile, or Block to stop seeing a user. Reports reach a person who reviews them and can take the content down.
How do I delete my account?
In the app: Profile → Profile settings → Delete Account. This removes your account and your data. The action cannot be undone.
Without the app: email ahmetozdemircy@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account and all personal data within 30 days.
The app cannot find events near me.
Location is optional and is only used on your device to work out your city. If you prefer, choose your city manually from the location picker instead of granting the permission.
