# Personal Data Protection

Dance Addict processes your personal data solely for the purposes described in this document, in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

---

## Data We Collect

| Category | Data Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Name and display name | Account creation and profile display |
| Contact | E-mail address | User authentication and notifications |
| Contact | Phone number (optional) | Instructor contact purposes |
| Event Data | Events and classes you create | Core app functionality |
| Reservation Data | Reservation requests and guest counts | Booking management |
| Technical | Device push token | Reservation and event notifications |
| Technical | IP address, device name, app usage logs | App stability and Firebase sync |

**We do NOT collect:**
- Photos or any visual/facial data (except posters you voluntarily upload)
- GPS or precise location data
- Device contacts
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
- Any biometric data
- Payment or financial information

---

## Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data on the following legal bases:

- **Contractual necessity** — to provide the Dance Addict service you signed up for
- **Legitimate interest** — to improve app performance and send relevant notifications
- **Consent** — for optional data such as phone numbers provided by instructors

---

## Data Sharing

We share your data only with:

- **Firebase (Google)** — for authentication, database, and push notifications
- **Other users** — your name and event/class details are visible to other app users as part of the service

We do not sell your data to third parties or use it for advertising purposes.

---

## Data Retention

We retain your personal data as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion (Profile → Delete Account), all your data is removed within 30 days.

---

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure** — delete your account and all associated data
- **Portability** — receive your data in a structured format
- **Objection** — object to certain types of processing

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@danceaddict.app

---

## Data Security

We implement commercially reasonable security measures to protect your data. All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in Google Firebase with authentication-based access controls.

---

## Changes to This Document

We may update this Data Protection notice from time to time. We will inform you of significant changes via the app.
