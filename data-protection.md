# Personal Data Protection

Dance Addict processes your personal data solely for the purposes described in this document, in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Last updated: **July 16, 2026**

---

## Data We Collect

| Category | Data Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Name and display name | Account creation and profile display |
| Identity | Sign in with Apple identifier | Authentication, if you sign in with Apple |
| Contact | E-mail address | User authentication and notifications |
| Contact | Phone / WhatsApp number (optional) | So dancers can contact organizers and instructors |
| Profile | Bio and dance style preferences (optional) | Profile display |
| Event Data | Events and classes you create, including poster images | Core app functionality |
| Reservation Data | Reservation requests and guest counts | Booking management |
| Safety Data | Reports you submit (reason, description, target) | Moderating objectionable content |
| Safety Data | List of users you have blocked | Hiding blocked users' events from you |
| Subscription | Organizer Pro subscription status and purchase history | Unlocking Pro features |
| Technical | Device push token | Reservation and event notifications |
| Technical | IP address and connection data (recorded by Firebase) | Operating the service and syncing your data |
| Technical | Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics), linked to your account identifier | Finding and fixing bugs |
| Usage | App usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): screens, sign-ins, events opened, reservation requests and events created, with an app instance ID and your account identifier | Understanding and improving the app |

Crash reports and usage statistics contain only IDs and categories, never your name, email address or notes. They are not used for advertising or to track you across other apps or websites.

### Location — used on your device only

If you allow location access, your approximate location (roughly one kilometre accuracy) is used **on your device** to detect your city, and only that city preference is saved on your device. Coordinates are never transmitted to or stored on our servers, and no location history is kept. Choosing your city manually from the list avoids location access entirely.

### We do NOT collect
- Location coordinates or location history on our servers
- Photos or any visual/facial data (except posters you voluntarily upload)
- Device contacts
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
- Any biometric data
- Payment card or bank details — purchases are processed entirely by Apple

---

## Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data on the following legal bases:

- **Contractual necessity** — to provide the Dance Addict service you signed up for, including any subscription you purchase
- **Legitimate interest** — to improve app performance, send relevant notifications, and keep the community safe by moderating reported content
- **Consent** — for optional data such as your phone number, profile bio, or location access

---

## Data Sharing

We share your data only with:

- **Firebase (Google)** — for authentication, database, storage, and push notifications
- **RevenueCat** — for Organizer Pro subscription status, linked to your account identifier
- **Apple** — for Sign in with Apple and for processing subscription purchases
- **Other users** — your name and event/class details are visible to other app users as part of the service

Reports you submit are shared only with our moderators. They are never shown to the person you reported. The list of users you block is visible only to you.

We do not sell your data to third parties or use it for advertising purposes.

---

## Data Retention

We retain your personal data as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion (**Profile → Profile settings → Delete Account**), all your data is removed within 30 days. If you signed in with Apple, deletion also revokes the app's Sign in with Apple token.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification** — correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure** — delete your account and all associated data
- **Portability** — receive your data in a structured format
- **Objection** — object to certain types of processing
